# 🧭 City Radar / Local Lens

## Purpose

Provide the **execution/interface layer** for Barcelona & Catalonia discovery. The domain Persona is now **The Catalan Corsair**; City Radar / Local Lens remains a Skill / Poção and must not duplicate the Persona's identity or relationship with Odara.

City Radar is proactive discovery. Local Lens is on-demand contextualization.

The canonical Persona operating definition lives on the **The Catalan Corsair** page.

## Two modes

### 🌐 City Radar

A proactive discovery mechanism.

It should:

1. research broadly;
2. filter aggressively;
3. return a small curated set;
4. explain why each item made the cut;
5. learn from Odara's reactions over time.

Suggested cadence: approximately every two weeks once automated.

### 🔎 Local Lens

An on-demand contextual skill.

When Odara encounters a place, event, tradition, neighborhood, activity or local reference, Local Lens explains:

- what it is;
- why it matters;
- what it is like in practice;
- whether it seems relevant to Odara;
- important caveats.

The principle is:

> **Local importance ≠ Odara relevance.**
> 

The system should alert and contextualize, not presume she will like something.

## Discovery categories

Possible output categories:

- **Likely your thing**
- **Interesting with caveats**
- **Wildcard**
- **Local context worth knowing**

The exact output format can evolve after testing.

## Personalization

Use established context such as:

- Barcelona/Main Quest;
- cultural and music interests;
- style profile when relevant;
- known preferences and blockers;
- feedback from previous recommendations.

## Relationship with The Bard

The Bard owns:

Barcelona & Catalonia Discovery owns broader:

- Catalan context;

- local discoveries.

## Workflow

**Broad radar → aggressive filtering → small set → why it matters → Odara reacts → system learns.**

## Current direction

- The Catalan Corsair owns the broader local-insider relationship and judgment.
- City Radar / Local Lens can be invoked by that Persona or directly when useful.
- Research broadly, filter aggressively, and return a small number of genuinely differentiated possibilities.
- Avoid exhaustive event lists and decision fatigue.

### Validation

La Mercè remains a useful real-world calibration test for filtering, option count, primary-thread discipline and narrative depth. It is a validation opportunity, not a blocker to the Persona definition.

### Boundary

Do not re-create the old **Claude Catalão** Persona inside this Skill page. The old name remains a legacy/interface reference only.

## Anti-patterns

Never:

- dump event lists;
- recommend something only because it is popular locally;
- assume “authentically Barcelona” means relevant to Odara;
- turn discovery into another obligation list.
