    - **Ad hoc** — surfacing as life happens (something new comes up, an obligation needs handling)

- 🏆 Progression · from 01 A Bússula, 2026-11-04
    
    *Why: same XP and streaks idea as above. Conflicts with the Memory Log as the main record and with "nothing becomes a score".*
    
    The Memory Log is the primary progression system — the real record of a life being lived. XP below is kept as an optional, lightweight layer alongside it, not a replacement: game mechanics should emerge naturally and support the experience, never dominate it.
    
    - **XP** *(optional layer)* — completing an Obligation or Quest grants XP; Bosses grant more than routine Obligations. Purely a light texture, not the point.
    - **Streaks** *(optional layer)* — tracked per recurring Obligation (e.g. the daily walk), broken gently — a missed day pauses the streak but doesn't erase its history
    - **Category balance** *(optional layer)* — a lightweight signal (not a score) showing which life areas have gotten attention recently vs. which have been quiet, so imbalance is visible without becoming another guilt trip
    - **Milestones** — unlocked at meaningful thresholds (first month of daily walks, first Boss defeated, etc.), celebrated rather than just logged — these can live in either the Memory Log or as a light XP marker, whichever fits the moment

- 🚦 Check-in Types · from 01 A Bússula, 2026-11-04
    
    *Why: the Bom Dia description is outdated, the "known limitation" is outdated, and Ad Hoc and Pit Stop duplicate the scripts now on the Skills page.*
    
    Four different triggers, each with a different job — treating them as one thing would blur what each is actually for.
    
    **Bom Dia (Daily Light Touch)** — triggered simply by her saying good morning / oi at the start of her day. Has a dedicated home now: the **"Bom dia Claude"** chat, which runs the full sequence documented in [[ARCHIVE] Bom Dia Claude (Rotina Matinal Completa)](To%20be%20trashed/%5BARCHIVE%5D%20Bom%20Dia%20Claude%20(Rotina%20Matinal%20Completa)%203af107b19b49811eab20ef076172f85d.md) — reminders (birthdays, priority items) → Gmail sweep → a superficial world-news round (Brazil/Spain/Barcelona-focused, deeper only if she asks). If she says good morning in some *other* chat instead, the lighter fallback still applies: just the reminders piece (birthdays, 🔺 priority items, near-term deadlines) — no Gmail, no world news, no Wildcard, no Mood. Either way, this stays lighter in spirit than a full Ad Hoc Check-in.
    
    **Ad Hoc Check-in** — player-initiated, whenever she opens the chat *asking for a real check-in* (not just a good-morning). Full GM treatment: Cadence Queue + Wildcard Side Quest + optional Mood/Flavor. This is what the GM Check-in Script (v1) already implements.
    
    **Deadline Alert** — triggered by a Due Date approaching (e.g. rent due the 5th). Minimal narrative on purpose — its only job is making sure something urgent isn't missed, not entertaining her. Still respects Boss framing if the Obligation qualifies, but otherwise stays short and practical.
    
    **Weekly Pit Stop (Mondays)** — a different kind of session: part retrospective, part preview. Reviews last week's due items (done / missed-and-rescheduled / no longer relevant) and previews what Recurrence says is coming this week, so Status never goes stale. Bigger in scope than a daily check-in, but doesn't generate its own Wildcard Quest — it's a planning ritual, not a play session.
    
    *(Known limitation: Claude/[claude.ai](http://claude.ai) has no native way to message her unprompted at a set time — there's no true "ping at 11am." Bom Dia is the practical workaround: since she opens a chat most mornings anyway, that moment becomes the trigger instead of a clock. Claude Cowork's Scheduled Tasks feature may eventually offer true automation — in beta, rolling out by plan tier as of 2026-08-01 — worth revisiting later, not a priority now.)*
