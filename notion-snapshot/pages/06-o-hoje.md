<b>06 · O HOJE — The Living Present</b>

<b>Where the ship is right now.</b>

Odyssey already knows why it sails, how the ship works, what it looks like, who is aboard and what happened along the way. <b>O Hoje</b> is the missing sixth pillar: the present tense of Odara's life — the open things, the moving things, and the things waiting for the world to answer.

<b>Purpose:</b> be the home of the living operational state that Odyssey reads and writes every day, and make it possible to understand that state without digging through databases.

<b>Current readiness:</b> narrative and structure defined; relocation is partly done, and the sources listed here are <b>not yet connected into live views</b>. The three windows below are a designed surface, not real-time status indicators.
</callout>

<quote color="green_bg">
<b>A Bússola é para onde eu vou. A Jornada é o rastro atrás do navio. O Hoje é onde o navio está agora.</b>
</quote>

<b>The present is not another plan to maintain. It is a position to recognize.</b> A good visit to O Hoje should answer one question: <b>“Where am I, and what matters from here?”</b>

<callout icon="🤖" color="purple_bg">
## 01 · AI START HERE — Read the waterline
</callout>

1. <b>Observe before recommending.</b> Read the relevant live records. Never infer that a deadline was met, a task is complete or a reminder was delivered merely because a row exists.
2. <b>Orient the Captain in three signals:</b> what needs attention (🔺), what is in motion (🌊), and what is waiting (⏳). Show only the meaningful few, not an exhaustive to-do list.
3. <b>Follow the owner.</b> O Hoje owns the <i>location and visibility of current state</i>, not each domain's decisions. Gibbs owns the task ledger's hygiene; the responsible Persona owns its domain entry.
4. <b>Never confuse intent with execution.</b> A future view, automation or Command Center integration is a proposal until tested. If a source is unreachable or stale, say so clearly.
5. <b>Close the loop at the right home.</b> When a cycle or item is genuinely finished, use the existing domain rules to close it. Significant history belongs in <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/3fe107b19b4980a3b94de83b692dd5fb">05 A Jornada</mention>; do not accumulate a second archive here or auto-migrate records.

<b>Authority:</b> the <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/3aa107b19b4981079aaecf1f72b1bb98">Odyssey Operating Manual</mention> governs permissions; current domain rules govern recurrence, deadlines, eligibility and closure. <b>O Hoje shows. It does not decide.</b>

## 🧩 O problema que este pilar resolve

O Odyssey tem cinco pilares e nenhum deles é a vida da Odara <b>como ela está hoje</b>. Todos falam do sistema: por que ele existe, como foi construído, como se parece, quem o opera, o que já aconteceu.

A consequência prática é que o <b>dado vivo</b> — o que o sistema lê e escreve todo dia — nunca teve casa. E dado sem casa se perde:

- O Backlog de Tarefas estava dentro de <b>🗂️ Archive / To be trashed</b> e foi resgatado em 11/11/2026. Hoje está na última linha do <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/40c107b19b498031b5e5f5fa01cc84b6">02 O Navio</mention>, onde a Odara não acha.
- Os três Registros do Bom Dia estão <b>fora do projeto</b>, no topo do workspace.
- Isso causou aproximadamente <b>40 execuções falhas</b> do Bom Dia Claude, com erros de "base inacessível". Uma obrigação 🔺 de status legal venceu sem nunca aparecer.

O diagnóstico: essas bases não são documentação (os pilares) nem conhecimento de domínio (o Source of Truth Mapping). São <b>estado</b>. E estado precisava de um lugar.
