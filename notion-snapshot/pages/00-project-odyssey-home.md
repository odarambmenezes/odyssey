<p>
The world doesn't need another productivity system. There are already countless apps for tasks, calendars, habits and reminders. Most of them fail for the same reason:

<b>They assume life is predictable, when in reality, it can be anything but.</b>
</p>

Trying to perfectly control everything in life will always lead to <u>frustration</u>. Odyssey starts from a different assumption:

<quote color="green_bg">
<b>Chaos isn't the enemy, it's the fun part of the journey.</b>
</quote>

- Instead of trying to eliminate unpredictability, Odyssey uses chaos to recalculate <u>narrative</u>.
- Instead of maximizing productivity, Odyssey prioritizes <u>experiences</u>, emotions, curiosity, and the moments that make life meaningful.
- Instead of treating money, power, productivity, and status as the ultimate measures of success, Odyssey questions those expectations and makes room for a more personal definition of a life well lived.
- <b>Odyssey is about making more room for life, not turning life into more work.</b>
  - The goal isn't to organize every minute or turn life into a perfectly optimized schedule. It is to create a system that serves Odara, taking on the mental effort of remembering, organizing, filtering, and curating information so she can focus on what actually matters to her.

## The Captain's Deck — <i>Your ship at a glance!</i>
</callout>

<callout icon="🧭" color="green_bg">
<b>LIVE · MVP since 27 Nov 2026:</b> [Open the Captain's Deck](https://claude.ai/artifact/QrhEpSQEGxgaM4voXoWUqf)

Odyssey's interactive home base, built by Hacker. Rearrangeable widgets (move, resize, minimize, remove) with live data from Notion and Google Calendar: today, agenda, tickets with quick links into the cards, tasks, quests, islands, Crew, Noticeboard, personal Sources of Truth, Skills, Radar de Shows, documentation audit, recent decisions, documentation and databases. Each screen size (phone, tablet, desktop/TV) keeps its own arrangement, synced through Odara's account. Notion stays the source of truth and the fallback.
</callout>

<quote color="green_bg">
<b>The Captain doesn't need to see every moving part of the ship — only what helps her choose where to sail next.</b>
</quote>

<b>One glance. One clear next action. Back to life.</b>

The Captain's Deck will bring together only the information that deserves a place on the main deck: who's aboard, what matters now, what needs attention and which tools can actually help.

<b>Design rule:</b> show the useful summary here, then open the existing Source of Truth for details. No duplicated records, competing dashboards or extra maintenance for the Captain.

<b>The live-state home behind this future window:</b> <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/416107b19b4981f4beeeecc87e51b8bd">06 O Hoje</mention> — the new sixth pillar. Its Current Tide is still a designed surface, not a live dashboard.

🛠️ Build direction — MVP first, connected production later

<b>MVP:</b> start with a lightweight version using verified links, simple views and a few useful cards. Test the connections that matter before adding complexity. Label any example data clearly as a placeholder.

<b>Future production:</b> evolve into an always-on, iPad-friendly dashboard with selected live updates and eventually rearrangeable cards. Plan to reuse the MVP's sources, and fall back to the original Notion pages if an integration is unavailable.

<b>Keep on the main page only:</b> information that helps the Captain make a decision or take action immediately. Detailed instructions and system specifications stay in their canonical homes; meaningful history stays in Jornada, and retired original documentation leaves the project under G21.

<b>In the MVP (27 Nov 2026):</b> live reads, refresh every 10 minutes, drag-and-drop widgets and layout sync across devices. <b>Not yet:</b> Quick Actions that run Skills from the deck, and a Radars feed beyond the Skills Mapping status.
</toggle>
