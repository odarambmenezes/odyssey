## 03 · Decisions & Learnings — The why behind the work
</callout>

<b>Record meaningful decisions with context:</b> what was decided, why, by whom, what changed, what was tried and what was learned. Link to the resulting current page rather than copying its rules.

<b>Status matters:</b> Proposed → Approved → Implemented → Validated, with Superseded / Archived when applicable. Approval alone does not imply successful implementation.

<callout icon="🔄" color="purple_bg">
## 05 · Knowledge Lifecycle — Remember, reconcile, release
</callout>

1. <b>Capture selectively:</b> preserve the decision, lesson or memory with source and context when useful. Chat alone is not durable documentation.
2. <b>Classify:</b> confirmed vs inferred, proposed vs approved, active vs historic, implemented vs validated.
3. <b>Reconcile before promotion:</b> verify ownership, approval, scope, contradictions and current authority. Recent historical text does not automatically trump a higher-authority source.
4. <b>Give it one home:</b> active domain knowledge belongs to its <b>Source of Truth</b>; project-wide rules to the <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/3aa107b19b4981079aaecf1f72b1bb98">Odyssey Operating Manual</mention> or responsible pillar; <b>live operational state</b> to <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/416107b19b4981f4beeeecc87e51b8bd">06 O Hoje</mention>; session history to Session Log; unresolved items to <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/1ee7ad29377949e2af70cb3ca6c3faa6" type="database">Grab & Go</mention>; legacy to the offline Odyssey archive.
5. <b>Preserve or retire deliberately:</b> link to the integrated result and retain meaningful provenance; archive/delete only under applicable permissions. A staging area is not a final archive.
