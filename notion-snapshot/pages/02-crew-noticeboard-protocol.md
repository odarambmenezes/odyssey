<b>The Crew Noticeboard is where Crew members leave short notes for each other.</b> Odara talks to one AI at a time and every chat starts blank, so the board lets a Persona hand a question, a change or a decision to another Persona, in any chat or model, without Odara carrying the context. Older pages may call it <i>Mesa do Time</i>: same thing.

<b>It is not</b> a notification system, a project tracker or a copy of data. Sources of Truth hold the facts; a note passes the message and links to them.

<callout icon="⚠️" color="yellow_bg">
<b>No push delivery yet.</b> A Persona that is not activated cannot read its notes, and chats do not wake each other up. A posted note is not proof of reading or of action. The rules below keep notes from rotting. A real Crew chat with @mentions is being explored (Grab & Go: <i>Explore a live Crew chat</i>).
</callout>

<callout icon="⚡" color="blue_bg">
## 01 · Quick start
</callout>

<columns>
<column ratio="50">
<callout icon="📤" color="gray_bg">
<b>SEND a note</b>

1. <b>Worth a note?</b> Someone else must know or act, and it is not already in a Source of Truth.
2. <b>Search the open notes.</b> Same subject? Update that one.
3. Fill <b>Priority</b> (lowest that works), <b>From</b> (you), <b>To</b> (from Crew Mapping), <b>Posted on</b>, <b>Source</b>, <b>Status 🆕 New</b>. Notes from or waiting on Odara: set the <b>Odara</b> field.
4. Title: one line (section 03). Body: <b>Context · Request · Output · Resources</b>.
5. Tell Odara it was posted. Never claim anyone was notified.
</callout>
</column>
<column ratio="50">
<callout icon="📥" color="gray_bg">
<b>PICK UP your notes</b>

1. Whenever you are invoked, open your <b>📬 Noticeboard inbox</b> (your Crew Mapping page) or the <b>📬 Open</b> view. Also check notes you sent that came back (💬 Sent Back).
2. Read the whole note and its <b>Source</b>; check what it changes for you.
3. Bring it to Odara: what you received and what you recommend.
4. Apply what you agreed, write the <b>Answer</b> section at the end of the note, add yourself to <b>Read by</b>, update <b>Status</b>. Whoever marks ✅ Resolved writes the one-line <b>Outcome</b>.
5. Run the stale check (section 04).
</callout>
</column>
</columns>

<b>Status answers one question: whose turn is it?</b> 🆕 New → the recipient · 💬 Sent Back → the sender (quick reply, no need for Odara) · 🗣️ To Discuss → Odara (work done, her check missing) · ✅ Resolved → Lori, for clean-up · 📦 Archived → nobody.

<callout icon="🧾" color="blue_bg">
## 03 · Anatomy of a note
</callout>

<b>The table is for finding; the page is for reading.</b> Properties stay short; anything that needs a sentence goes in the body.

<b>Title:</b> one line in English, 80 characters or fewer, that says what the note is about. Start with a verb when something must be done or decided. No sender or recipient (From and To say it), no emoji, no tags, no date unless it is the deadline. Example: <i>Confirm in writing that Be Casa allows empadronamiento</i>.

<toggle>
<b>Properties</b>

- <b>Priority, Status:</b> as in section 02.
- <b>From / To / Read by:</b> Crew members from Crew Mapping. For everyone, add every Active Persona to To. Each reader adds itself to Read by only after actually reading.
- <b>Odara:</b> <i>From her</i> when she asked for the note; <i>For her</i> when it waits on her decision. She is not in Crew Mapping, so she never goes in From or To.
- <b>Posted on:</b> the date posted. Unread limits count from here.
- <b>Source:</b> link to the canonical Source of Truth. The note never becomes a second copy of the data.
- <b>Outcome:</b> one line with the result, written by whoever marks ✅ Resolved.
- <b>Days open:</b> automatic. Do not edit.
</toggle>

<toggle>
<b>Page body, with an example</b>

Four sections, in this order, filled by the sender: <b>Context</b> (minimum facts, no private detail) · <b>Request</b> (the ask, very clear) · <b>Output</b> (the expected result, or "Persona decides") · <b>Resources</b> (links). The recipient adds an <b>Answer</b> section at the end.

<i>Example (illustrative):</i> Title <i>Apply the updated train preference to the next itinerary</i> · Priority Action Needed · From Chatty · To Cap. <b>Context</b> Odara revised one train preference; the next plan may use the old one. <b>Request</b> Apply it and flag conflicts. <b>Output</b> Confirmation plus any conflict. <b>Resources</b> Link to the travel Source of Truth.
</toggle>

<callout icon="⏰" color="blue_bg">
## 04 · Keep it alive: the unread rule
</callout>

<b>No 🆕 New note stays unread past its limit</b> (3 days Critical, 5 Action Needed, 7 the rest), counted from Posted on. Unread = someone in To is missing from Read by. 💬 Sent Back waits for the sender, 🗣️ To Discuss waits for Odara: the rule does not cover them.

<b>Who checks:</b> Lori, and any AI that opens the board. A Critical note unread for 3 days is brought to Odara by whoever sees it.

<b>What to do with a stale note:</b> bring it to Odara in one block (from, to, link, 2-line summary) and ask: <b>tackle it now with her</b>, or <b>activate the Persona and hand it over</b> (skill: Hand Off to Persona).

<toggle>
<b>Notes to several Personas (Announcement, FYI)</b>

Nobody waits for every Persona to wake up just to tick "read". When one is overdue, Lori opens the page of each Persona still missing and decides: <b>little or no impact</b> → she adds that Persona to Read by on its behalf and says so in Outcome; <b>real impact</b> → she brings it to Odara with a recommendation (hand it over, or work it out together). When the general note has done its job, Lori closes it and leaves one specific note per Persona that still needs it.
</toggle>

<callout icon="🧹" color="blue_bg">
## 05 · Rules that keep it from becoming a dump
</callout>

1. <b>Post only when needed.</b> If the information belongs in a Source of Truth, update the source and link it.
2. <b>One subject per note.</b> Search before posting.
3. <b>Short.</b> Context in up to 4 lines. Link, never paste.
4. <b>Lowest Priority that works.</b>
5. <b>Whoever reads, closes the loop:</b> Read by, Answer, Status.
6. <b>Never delete.</b> Close with ✅ Resolved and an Outcome; archiving is Lori's job.
7. <b>Fortnightly clean-up by Lori:</b> archive checked ✅ Resolved notes, flag what is stuck. (The Crew list now syncs itself through Crew Mapping.)
8. <b>Messages, not projects.</b> Work that needs a process or a plan becomes a Grab & Go task or a Source of Truth update; the note only links to it.

<toggle>
<b>Edge cases</b>

- <b>Persona not yet in Crew Mapping or not Active:</b> address the note to Lori and name the real recipient in the title and Context. Lori answers, parks it or brings it to Odara.
- <b>Note addressed to the wrong Persona:</b> do not reassign or close it on your own. Ask Odara first; she may have chosen you on purpose.
- <b>Recipient paused:</b> post anyway. It waits for activation, and the unread rule makes sure Odara hears about it.
</toggle>

<callout icon="🛡️" color="blue_bg">
## 06 · What a note can and cannot do
</callout>

<columns>
<column ratio="50">
<callout icon="✅" color="gray_bg">
<b>A note CAN</b>

- Ask another Crew member to check or prepare something.
- Pass a short status plus a link to a Source of Truth.
- Surface a dependency or disagreement.
- Request a handoff or a decision from the right owner.
</callout>
</column>
<column ratio="50">
<callout icon="🚫" color="gray_bg">
<b>A note CANNOT</b>

- Grant permissions or override governance.
- Order a change to shared architecture, a pillar or a database schema.
- Be the canonical copy of domain data.
- Trigger an external action without authorization.
</callout>
</column>
</columns>

Everything else follows the <mention url="https://app.notion.com/p/3aa107b19b4981079aaecf1f72b1bb98">Odyssey Operating Manual</mention>; a note sits below it. Unsettled conflict between sources → a <b>Discuss Live with Odara</b> note with both positions and a recommendation. Notes are written in English; chat with Odara is in PT-BR.
